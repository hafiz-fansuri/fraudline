What does inference receive?

Amount, relative event time, product code, card network, card type and whether identity data exists. Optional device, email-domain, region, earlier transactions and contextual signals add detail.

The backend derives 54 features. It never receives the fraud label. Amounts here use the IEEE dataset's USD convention; other currencies need a validated mapping.

How do I import transactions?

Download the example JSON, keep its field names and import an object containing a transactions list. Maximum 100 transactions and 256 KiB. This public demo is for synthetic data only.

What does Proofline contribute?

A bounded evidence workflow: inspect the risk findings, retrieve relevant playbooks, and return cited passages and human review steps. Ask assistant uses a free OpenRouter model to answer your question. It distinguishes cited playbook guidance from general explanations. Playbooks are illustrative, not approved bank policy.

Is this ready for customer banking data?

No. This public demo has no account authentication or tenant isolation. Production needs bank-approved policies, trusted inputs, access controls, durable audit storage, validation and operational review.
