Clear answers.
No fine-print surprises.

Is this checking a real payment?+

No. This is an interactive demonstration using synthetic transactions and example histories. It does not connect to a bank, charge a card, or approve or stop a payment. Please do not enter personal or payment information.

Where does the risk estimate come from?+

A LightGBM model trained using the IEEE-CIS fraud benchmark estimates probability. It is evaluated on later held-out transactions. Location, account and other contextual checks are separate policies; they may change the suggested action without changing the model probability.

Does a warning mean it is definitely fraud?+

No. Legitimate activity can look unusual. The model's selected review threshold flags about 19.3% of legitimate transactions in its held-out test. A warning is a reason to verify details, not an accusation or a guarantee.

Are my checks saved?+

Recent checks are shown only in this browser session and disappear when you reload. The public service also writes a temporary scoring record that may disappear when its instance restarts. This is not a private or durable customer account. Use example values only.
